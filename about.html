About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Program in Modern Thought and Literature at Stanford University, with minors in Computer Science and Communication, and a Graduate Certificate in Science, Technology, and Society. My research examines videogames, gender, and digital platforms in China, specifically focusing on algorithmic bias and platform governance. As an amateur game designer, I adopt a critical design approach to explore themes such as reproductive rights and gun violence. My work has appeared in Game Studies, Feminist Media Studies, ROMchip, and Platypus, and a chapter is included in the proposed edited volume Asian Popular Culture and the Gothic (Routledge Advances in Popular Culture Studies series).
